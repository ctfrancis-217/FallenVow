# Fallen Vow

Fallen Vow is a pixel-art roguelite where a fallen paladin turned Black Knight and his descendants fight through the Demon Lord's castle. Play at https://fallenvow.com. Controls: A/D move, Space jump, W/S aim, left mouse attack, 1-4 abilities, Shift dash, E interact, Esc pause, F fullscreen.
Touch controls: left-thumb joystick (up aims up; down aims down, drops through planks, pogo, or sweep); right thumb ATK, JUMP, DASH, 1 Spell, 2 Aegis, 4 Potion; E appears to interact; II pauses. Tap menu buttons and map nodes directly.
